## Mandatory user constraints
- **Search globally. Do not exclude because of Egypt by default.** Country is relevant only where a specific provider's **actual** registration, banking, payout, tax or licensing policies block the opportunity.
- Accepted existing or potentially accessible payout channels: PayPal, Payoneer (previously mentioned), Airtm, RedotPay, InstaPay, **Binance Pay**, and supported crypto transfers. Payer must explicitly support a channel and receiver must be eligible.
- Binance Pay is a Binance **custodial payment route** via Pay ID / QR / funding wallet, **not** a self-custody wallet for signing arbitrary on-chain contracts. For external on-chain USDC/USDT payouts, a compatible supported Binance crypto **deposit address and network** could receive payment, subject to supported network/token/memo/minimums and provider terms. Binance's separate Web3 Wallet is self-custodial, optional.
- $10 is maximum exploratory risk budget, not a mandatory stake. No spending, paid subscriptions, deposits, crypto signing, applying/submitting, or outbound messages without explicit approval.
- Prefer no calls, no AI-training work, no security/coding bounties, no referrals/affiliate funnels, no contests, no cold client acquisition or Upwork proposal competition. Work on computer/phone with repeatable clear deliverables and externally verified payouts.
- Target remains fast meaningful cash (aspirational $100/day or $1,000/week), but DO NOT assert that an available platform can guarantee it. First prove actual ability to register, receive tasks, have completed work approved and withdraw money.

## Payment method distinction
- Binance Pay supports receiving via QR, Binance UID / phone / email; credited to Funding wallet. That is a Binance-internal payment service; outside websites do not automatically support paying to a Binance UID.
- External crypto payment requires compatible **coin, blockchain, address** and sometimes memo, and network support at both payer and Binance.
- PayPal / Payoneer payment is independent of Binance Pay. Geographic restrictions should be researched for each **specific** service; never use region alone as unsupported veto.
