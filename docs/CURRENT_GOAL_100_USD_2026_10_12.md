## Constraints retained

Online/global reach, no reliance on cold client acquisition, Upwork bid competition, traditional jobs, AI-training tasks, referrals, contests or promises of extraordinary trading return. No wallet signing, deposits, authorizations, withdrawals, conversions, bids, commitments or GitHub production merges without explicit approval. Read-only GitHub research branches and draft PRs may be used for documented investigation. Do not upload keys, private wallet data or confidential user data.

## Decision threshold

The $900 net earning gap over one month cannot be met with an ordinary 1%-to-10% annual yield on $100. A public 'reward formula' is insufficient. A candidate becomes actionable only if it has a currently funded, independently confirmed and transferable caller reward; a live callable opportunity; correct source+deployment; conservative net economics (gas, other fees and token liquidity); and acceptable competition/capture feasibility. Use on-chain simulation as necessary but never mistake it for guaranteed mining.
