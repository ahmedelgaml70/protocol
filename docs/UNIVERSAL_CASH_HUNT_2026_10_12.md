# Universal Cash Hunt — research pivot (12 October 2026)

## Hard goal, honest status

Discover a **lawful, independently collectible, already-funded** way for one individual to earn money online with **≤$10 total at-risk capital**, potentially receive funds via available mainstream payment rails, and pursue an aspirational **$1,000** as quickly as reality allows. No jobs, freelance services, manual microtasks, outreach to clients, marketing, referrals, paid subscriptions, gambling, stolen property, unconsented asset recovery, exploiting security vulnerabilities, or breaking platform terms.

**Current result: $0 verified realizable new income. No $1,000 shortcut located.** A posted reward, marketplace quote, theoretical arbitrage, cloud credit, simulation, and somebody else's claim are not earnings. Do not risk user's $10 without express, separate approval.

## Economic framework — find the missing subsidy

Permissionless money is paid for something: scarce capital, work, access to an asset, accepting economic risk, performing publicly valuable upkeep, or locating a funded entitlement where the original owner has consented. A system that pays anyone for zero-cost, zero-risk actions is ordinarily rapidly claimed; this is why a 100× immediate return cannot be assumed.

Search for **temporary structural mistakes or time mismatches**, not generic "passive-income websites." Require one of: protocol treasury/incentive pool already deposited; cross-venue best **executable** bids/asks after fees; public maintenance incentive with verified paid outsider; capacity-sharing rewards with realistic yield; bona fide claims to funds belonging to the claimant.

## Search gate: no false opportunities

1. **Source of money**: whose funds, how much, currently escrowed/funded, and rights of claimant; verify entitlement is public or belongs to user.
2. **No-customer/no-task**: does the payout truly require no client outreach, promotion, listing/buyer, or paid labor? Otherwise reject under current constraints.
3. **Execution**: what deterministic condition triggers payment? Is it *currently true*? Is there permissionless access and a reproducible low-risk simulation?
4. **Real-money settlement**: historical evidence of **actual outsider receipt**, cash-out path available from Egypt, minimum withdrawal, KYC, hold, account terms.
5. **Economics**: at most $10 can be put at risk; quote net after every fee, timeout, slippage, failed attempts, transfer hold, taxes where material. Screen for **$50+ per occasion** before building new automation; this is a research prioritization threshold, not a claimed opportunity.
6. **Competition and time**: how many operators, first-come-first-served, effective access latency, priority payments, and realistic days-to-cash.
7. **Pre-approval**: no spending, account changes, asset purchases, financial deposits, or signed transactions without explicit confirmation.

**Failure to establish even one gate:** not execution-ready. Need direct current *positive* evidence, not a blog post that claims earnings.

## Better use of automation

Keep existing public GitHub research bots for protocol/market signals but do **not** spend another programming session on a candidate without proof of real funded payouts and credible $50+ net opportunity. A new **universal mechanical-rewards monitor** can watch different industries, but it should not be built until at least one concrete, independently verified payout source supports a stable read-only query or API. Avoid building a giant dashboard that only measures theoretical "opportunities."
