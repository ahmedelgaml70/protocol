## Track B: Reusable digital product without cold customer acquisition

The user's established combination of original editable/animated PowerPoint know-how, video assets, MSK/clinical knowledge and low-cost coding suggests **one differentiated premium downloadable education asset**, not copying generic templates. Potential pilot concept: **native editable anatomy and rehabilitation motion slide kit** for educators, with functioning stepwise animations and teaching notes. A more general presentation-motion kit might reach a larger audience. These are *hypotheses*, **not validated demand**.

**Market research:** Etsy currently displays ~86+ items for 'anatomy PowerPoint', hundreds for 'medical PowerPoint template', and more than 1,000 for 'physiotherapy template'. Some indexed listings show hundreds of reviews but they are *NOT* evidence of current weekly unit sales for our proposed specialized motion kit. Generic medical slide products are often priced around $2–$10 and are competitive, so we must prove buyers will pay a premium for **editable high-quality animations**, rather than assuming a $30 sale. Product originality/rights must be verified.

**Market access/payments:**
- **Etsy now explicitly includes Egypt** in Etsy Payments via Payoneer, contrary to older assumptions. However the official opening process requires a **one-time nonrefundable setup fee**, unknown until registration, plus $0.20/listing, 6.5% transaction fee and an Egypt payment processing fee listed as 6.5% + $0.30. Given a strict $10 capital budget, **do NOT open a shop or spend any fee without seeing exact cost and obtaining approval**. The official listings indicate visible buyer interest but not first-week sales certainty.
- **Gumroad** permits bank payout to Egypt in EGP, but regular withdrawals have **$100 minimum** and **7-day holding period** before payout, plus bank processing delays. Not a rapid-payout plan unless sales arrive and threshold is met. Upfront product-listing cost is not shown as mandatory.
- **itch.io** offers direct-to-PayPal sales with payments arriving to the payment processor promptly, but its audience is primarily game assets and code. Anatomical PPT products may have poor discoverability; a game/education animation asset pack would require separate buyer validation. Without an existing trusted PayPal account, cannot assume access to payout.
- **Canva Creators** requires acceptance into a beta program. Its updated 2026 contributor policy says AI-generated content is not permitted, an important constraint on proposed automated asset creation. Avoid assuming AI-generated assets may be monetized there.
