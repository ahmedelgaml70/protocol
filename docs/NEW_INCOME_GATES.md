# Gates for new income opportunities — effective 2026-10-12

Do not promote a candidate to "ready to earn" unless ALL conditions below are supported by fresh, inspectable sources:

1. **Legitimate payer / platform:** independent evidence of actual approved worker payments or paid customer orders, not self-published '$1k in one week' marketing.
2. **Eligibility:** currently accepts an Egypt-resident without misrepresenting identity, location, experience or using a VPN; accepting registrations is NOT equal to accessible paid task inventory.
3. **Task access / demand:** directly visible, CURRENT open paid assignments at published rates OR recent paid sales/very specific near-match product demand. An empty application waitlist or old earnings screenshot FAILS.
4. **Payout:** official supported cash-equivalent Egypt payout rail, currency, minimum threshold, hold and KYC, platform fees, and cash access *within user's timeframe*; gift cards are not USD-equivalent cash automatically.
5. **Cost / economics:** account opening and listing costs <= $10 only with explicit approval; conservatively subtract commissions, withdrawal fees, conversion, rejected work, hours required and unavailable assignments.
6. **No client hunting / calls / AI training / unsafe bank testing / security bounties / crypto speculation / referrals / contest dependence.** Contractor task work additionally conflicts with the strict original no-freelancing condition—must be separately approved as an exception.
7. **Realistic frequency:** payout rates are meaningless without observed task volume or buyers. A $5 playtest requires 200 accepted tests for $1,000 gross; an amount such as $0.50 per audio minute demands 2,000 PAID audio minutes and many more actual editing minutes.
8. **Never represent theoretical gross USD as realized profit.** Keep every rejection and source-date in the ledger.
9. **No external action without authorization:** no registering, applying, editing existing profiles, submitting tests, publishing listings, sending messages, spending $10 or signing transactions before user approval.
