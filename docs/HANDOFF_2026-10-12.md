## 5. Research methodology and evidence standards

Read [RESEARCH_PLAYBOOK.md](RESEARCH_PLAYBOOK.md) for the exact gate criteria, source standards, working routine, and result JSON schema. Minimal strict ladder:

- **G0:** Payer and funded escrow/budget/account verified (not announcement or screenshots).
- **G1:** Exact action/task and legal eligibility accessible to a new operator; not privileged/restricted.
- **G2:** At least one *recent actual cleared payout* with identifiable receiving party, amount, asset, date; external buyer conversion if not stablecoin.
- **G3:** An *unclaimed right NOW* opportunity at block/time, or assigned available task, and proof no one else has reserved/claimed it.
- **G4:** Conservative actual net and ability to complete/settle by Nov 12; winning odds, gas, bid/market fees, failures, locks, refunds, account/KYC, currency and payout rail.
- **G5:** Explicit owner permission before real-world account activity or spending; only then a capped execution experiment, proof via user receipt.
- **G6:** Repeatability supported by measured available inventory and individual revenue (not average TVL, APY, token volume, or published payout pool).

**Never label G0–G4 as profit.** \(income=0\) until user genuinely receives transferable cleared money. Always separate `zero events in successful bounded scan` from `RPC incomplete`, and `historical payout` from `present opportunity`.
